ABOUT THE ALBUM

The seasons turn.
The music stays.

Shunkashuto (春夏秋冬), meaning “four seasons,” is a 12-track album by SUPICA KASASAGI, released on July 24, 2026.

The album includes “Sabita Sharin,” “Yama ni Furu Ame,” “Maigo no Koe,” “Shukai Kido,” “Karafuru,” and “Nanashi no Hana,” alongside six Special Versions. Follow the songs through changing seasons, and let their sound and words linger.
